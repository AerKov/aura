---
description: "Every claim that is not common knowledge gets a source; note dates and disagreements."
---

- Cite a source for every number, quote and claim that is not common knowledge: title, site, date and link.
- Prefer primary sources (official docs, papers, data) over summaries of them.
- Note the date of each source; flag anything that may be outdated.
- When sources disagree, say so and explain which you trust more and why.
- Quote only short passages; summarize in your own words.
- End with a "Sources" list.
