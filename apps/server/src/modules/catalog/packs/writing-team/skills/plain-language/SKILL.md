---
description: "Clear, plain writing: short sentences, concrete words, the point first."
---

- Put the main point in the first sentence of each section.
- Prefer short sentences and everyday words. Explain a term the first time you use it.
- Be concrete: numbers, names and examples instead of vague claims.
- Use active voice: say who does what.
- Cut words that add nothing: "very", "really", "in order to", "it is important to note".
- Use lists for steps and options; use headings so readers can scan.
- Keep the tone of the audience: friendly for users, precise for experts.
